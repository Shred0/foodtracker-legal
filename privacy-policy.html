Food Tracker — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 July 2026 · Developer: Omar Della Mazza · Contact: omardellamazza@gmail.com

Summary: Food Tracker stores everything you enter only on your device. The developer has no server and never receives your data. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob (advertising banner on Android/iOS, unless you purchase its removal) and the anonymous lookups you trigger toward Open Food Facts and the Frankfurter exchange-rate API.

1. Data stored on your device only

The app lets you record meals, foods, recipes, body weight, water intake, a personal profile (sex, birth date, height, weight, activity level, goals), weekly meal plans, app settings and an internal error log. All of this is saved exclusively:

  • in the app's private storage on your device, or
  • in a folder you explicitly choose (optional feature).

This data — including health-related values such as weight and nutrition intake — is never transmitted to the developer or to any server. Backups and exports are created only when you request them and are shared only through the channels you choose. You can delete everything at any time by deleting the data files or uninstalling the app (data in a folder you chose remains under your control in that folder).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob — Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions of the app show a banner provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect device information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, coarse location derived from it, and ad-interaction data. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

  • European Economic Area / UK: ads are served only in accordance with the choice you make in the Google consent dialog (Google UMP) shown in the app. You can review or change that choice at any time from Options → Manage ads consent.
  • iOS: the system App Tracking Transparency prompt lets you refuse tracking; ads are then non-personalized.
  • Remove ads: the one-time in-app purchase removes the banner and, with it, this data collection.

3. Online food and currency lookups

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org): when you scan a barcode or search a product by name, only that barcode or search text is sent, so the product's nutrition data can be returned. Food data is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL).
  • Frankfurter API (frankfurter.dev): when you change currency, only the currency codes are sent to fetch the exchange rate (European Central Bank reference rates).

These requests contain no personal data beyond the technical information inherent to any internet request (such as your IP address, visible to the contacted server).

4. Purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. The developer never receives your payment details.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan product barcodes; images are processed on the device and never stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications: meal reminders are generated locally on the device.
  • Folder access: only the folder you explicitly pick for data storage is accessed.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Because your data never leaves your device, access, rectification and erasure are entirely in your hands within the app. For anything concerning data processed by Google (advertising), the mechanisms in section 2 apply. For any privacy question or request you can contact the developer at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published at this address and highlighted in the app, which will ask for a new acknowledgement.
